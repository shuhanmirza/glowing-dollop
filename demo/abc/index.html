Demo Identity Namespace

abc.localhost

This domain owns the abc.localhost email namespace. Its users (e.g. alice@abc.localhost) are authenticated by a third-party OpenID Provider, and this domain delegates a blind relay so its users can sign in to services it has no prior relationship with.

Machine-readable delegation is published at /.well-known/byoid-configuration — the document a Verifier fetches over TLS to learn this namespace's issuer and blind relay.
Bring-Your-Own-IdP demo · namespace owner (not a Verifier, not an OP)
